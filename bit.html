The Real-World Analogy

Computers Don't Have Ears; They Have Light Switches.

Inside your computer chip are billions of microscopic microscopic electric switches (transistors). They can only be in one of two physical states: OFF (0 volts) or ON (electric voltage). Every single letter, decimal number, 3D game graphic, and audio track is just an organized row of these switches.

Module 4

Why Does byte Only Go Up to 127? (Signed Odometer Effect)

Java integer primitives do not have unsigned types[cite: 1]. The leftmost bit (High-Order Bit) is reserved as the Sign Bit[cite: 1].

The Car Odometer Analogy

When a car odometer hits 999,999 and you drive 1 more mile, it rolls over to 000,000. In Java, when an 8-bit byte hits its ceiling of +127 (binary 01111111) and you add 1, the sign bit flips to 10000000, turning the number into -128!

• Bit 7 (Sign Bit): A 0 means positive, and a 1 means negative. That takes away half the numbers from positive range!

• Bits 0-6 (Value Bits): Max positive is 0111 1111 = 64+32+16+8+4+2+1 = 127.

• That is why a byte holds -128 to +127, NOT 0 to 255.
