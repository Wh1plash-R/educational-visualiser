Calculated Metrics & Step-by-Step Math

Formulas strictly derived from OS principles and course notes.

Formula: TT = Tend - At | WT = TT - BT
Job Arrival (At) Burst (BT) Completion Time (Tend) Turnaround Time (Tend - At) Waiting Time (TT - BT)
Averages: ttave = -- wtave = --

Turnaround Time (TT)

Total residence time spent in system from submission to completion:

TT = Completion Time (Tend) - Arrival Time (At)

Waiting Time (WT)

Total time spent idling in ready queue waiting for CPU:

WT = Turnaround Time (TT) - Burst Time (BT)

CPU Utilization

Fraction of elapsed time during which the processor was executing jobs:

Utilization = (Total Burst / Total Elapsed) * 100%
Intuitive Learning

Understanding CPU Scheduling with Everyday Life

Algorithms feel abstract until you connect them to what you experience every day: waiting at the grocery checkout, doctor visits, and restaurant orders.

🛒

FCFS = Supermarket Checkout Line

First-Come, First-Served

Whoever stands in line first gets scanned first. If person #1 has a full cart with 200 items (a huge CPU burst time of 13), everyone behind them with just a pack of gum must wait agonizingly long.

The "Convoy Effect": In our handout, J1 takes 13 units. All fast jobs (J2, J3) are stuck waiting behind the big truck!
⚡

SJF = Express Counter Preference

Shortest Job First

The cashier scans customers who only have 1 or 2 items first before moving to people with large baskets. This dramatically cuts the average wait time for the crowd!

The Risk (Starvation): What if quick customers keep walking in constantly? The customer with 100 items might wait forever!
🏥

Priority = Hospital Emergency Triage

Priority 1 = Critical Emergency

A patient arriving with a heart attack (Priority 1) skips past patients with a mild headache (Priority 5), even if the headache patient arrived hours earlier.

Non-Preemptive vs. Preemptive: In Non-Preemptive, if the doctor is currently bandaging an arm, they finish the bandage first. In Preemptive, the doctor drops the bandage immediately to treat the heart attack!
⏱️

Round Robin = Arcade / Chess Timer

Time Quantum = 3 Minutes

Each kid gets exactly 3 minutes on the arcade game. When the buzzer beeps, they must step off and walk to the end of the line if they still want more play time. No single kid can hog the console!

Why it feels responsive: Every process gets a turn very quickly, making modern multitasking and interactive desktops smooth.

The 5 Cardinal Rules of CPU Scheduling Performance

1. CPU Utilization
Keep CPU Busy

Goal: Maximize % of time CPU executes jobs instead of idling.

2. Throughput
More Jobs Finished

Goal: Maximize number of processes completed per unit time.

3. Turnaround Time
Shorter Residence

Goal: Minimize Tend - At (submission to complete).

4. Waiting Time
Less Queue Wait

Goal: Minimize time idling in the ready queue.

5. Response Time
Fast First Reaction

Goal: Minimize time until first response is produced.

Interprocess Communication (IPC) Mechanisms

Shared Memory

Fastest method. Processes read & write to a shared region of RAM directly.

Pipes & Named Pipes

One-way unidirectional streams. Named pipes allow unrelated processes to talk.

Message Queues

Processes post and retrieve messages from an OS-managed mailbox queue.

Sockets

Enables communication across different network machines via IP & port.

Signals

Asynchronous notification alerting a process that a specific event occurred.

Semaphores & Mutex

Integer counters and ownership locks preventing race conditions in critical code.
