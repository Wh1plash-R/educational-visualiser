Question 01 Multiple Choice
Which player motivation is defined by demonstrating the ability to dominate the game world and increase character skills to attain high scores and rankings?
A. Escapism
B. Mastery
C. Knowledge
D. Competition
Answer: B. Mastery
Mastery is demonstrated by increasing character skills to win and assessing status through high scores and rankings.
Question 02 Multiple Choice
Which market element consists of people's values, attitudes, and lifestyles?
A. Geographics
B. Demographics
C. Psychographics
D. Ergonomics
Answer: C. Psychographics
Psychographics consists of values, attitudes, and lifestyles, which are more relevant to personality and harder to guess externally.
Question 03 Multiple Choice
In the Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI), which dichotomy measures whether a person's source of information/perception is present-oriented (data) or future-oriented (ideas)?
A. Extrovert (E) vs. Introvert (I)
B. Sensing (S) vs. Intuitive (N)
C. Thinking (T) vs. Feeling (F)
D. Judging (J) vs. Perceiving (P)
Answer: B. Sensing (S) vs. Intuitive (N)
Sensing relies on data and present orientation, while Intuitive relies on ideas and future orientation.
Question 04 Multiple Choice
Which generation of players is associated with "latchkey" self-reliance and best connects with an independent, nomadic character who takes risks?
A. Silent Generation
B. Boom Generation
C. Generation X
D. Millennial Generation
Answer: C. Generation X
Generation X experienced falling fertility, divorce epidemic, and latchkey self-reliance, connecting with independent, risk-taking characters.
Question 05 Multiple Choice
Which generation came of age during the 1960s and 1970s spiritual revolution and responds best to games featuring social, spiritual, or political messages?
A. Boom Generation
B. Silent Generation
C. Generation X
D. Millennial Generation
Answer: A. Boom Generation
The Boom Generation started a society-wide, values-oriented spiritual revolution during their coming-of-age in the 1960s and 1970s.
Question 06 Multiple Choice
In the traditional three-act plot structure, which act comprises the bulk of the dramatic tension by focusing on obstacles the character must overcome?
A. Act I (Beginning)
B. Act II (Middle)
C. Act III (End)
D. Prologue
Answer: B. Act II (Middle)
Act II focuses on the series of obstacles standing in the way of solving the problem, comprising the bulk of the dramatic tension.
Question 07 Multiple Choice
A 1–2 sentence summary of a game's purpose and overall theme that appears on packaging to entice customers is the:
A. Backstory
B. Synopsis
C. Premise
D. Setting
Answer: C. Premise
The premise (or high concept) summarizes the game's purpose and overall theme in 1–2 sentences or a short paragraph.
Question 08 Multiple Choice
Which story element provides information leading up to where the game begins, usually found in the manual or as introductory text with voice-over?
A. Theme
B. Backstory
C. Plot
D. Premise
Answer: B. Backstory
Backstory provides information leading up to the start of the game, helping orient the player and establish initial bonds.
Question 09 Multiple Choice
Which storytelling technique draws players back to the main storyline by widening scope through subquests or introducing new characters?
A. Balancing conflict
B. Shifting focus
C. Suspension of disbelief
D. Realism
Answer: B. Shifting focus
Shifting focus draws players back to the main storyline while capturing interest through subquests and new unexplored areas.
Question 10 Multiple Choice
Which storytelling device causes players to forget real life and accept artificial rules that might not make sense in the real world?
A. Suspension of disbelief
B. Realism
C. Character arc
D. Unity of opposites
Answer: A. Suspension of disbelief
Suspension of disbelief refers to players accepting rules and experiences that contradict real-world logic.
Question 11 Multiple Choice
Which archetype represents the hero's opposite, often the ultimate evil character or the dark side of the hero?
A. Guardian
B. Shadow
C. Herald
D. Trickster
Answer: B. Shadow
The shadow represents the hero's opposite, often the ultimate evil character or the hero's dark side.
Question 12 Multiple Choice
Which archetype blocks the hero's progress by whatever means necessary until the hero has proven his or her worth?
A. Mentor
B. Ally
C. Guardian
D. Herald
Answer: C. Guardian
The guardian tests the hero and blocks progress (through riddles, combat, or internal blocks) until worth is proven.
Question 13 Multiple Choice
What narrative device links the protagonist and antagonist together when they both want the exact same thing?
A. Unity of opposites
B. Character triangle
C. Character arc
D. Balancing conflict
Answer: A. Unity of opposites
When both opposing characters desire the exact same goal or object, this device is known as the unity of opposites.
Question 14 Multiple Choice
Which type of antagonist is an anti-hero who could have been a protagonist, but whose destructive power leads to punishment and death to satisfy justice?
A. Mistaken antagonist
B. Transformational antagonist
C. Exaggerated antagonist
D. Realistic antagonist
Answer: B. Transformational antagonist
A transformational antagonist is an anti-hero who could have been a protagonist but must receive punishment/death at the end.
Question 15 Multiple Choice
According to the character arc levels based on Maslow's hierarchy, which level involves bonding with a small group such as an MMOG guild?
A. Level 1: Intrapersonal
B. Level 2: Interpersonal
C. Level 3: Team
D. Level 4: Community
Answer: C. Level 3: Team
At Level 3 (Team), the protagonist bonds with a small group with common interests, fulfilling the need to belong.

FALSE

Players seeking physical seclusion still interact socially with people in the privacy of their own physical environments.

FALSE

Act I captures attention and introduces the character's problem immediately.

FALSE

FALSE

Supporting (pivotal) characters exist primarily to prevent the protagonist from walking away and jump-start the action.

NPCs are not controlled by players; they are created and controlled by the game's AI engine.

In character development levels, Level 5 (Humanity) involves what Abraham Maslow calls self-actualization.

Level 5 (Humanity) represents spiritual growth and self-actualization once acceptance across a larger community is achieved.
